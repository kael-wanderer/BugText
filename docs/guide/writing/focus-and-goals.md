title: Focus, goals and spelling
summary: Write in focus mode, set a word goal, and let macOS check your spelling.

When the job is to write, BugText can hide everything else, keep your line steady on screen, and show how close you are to a word count.

## When to use it

- You are drafting and want fewer distractions.
- You have a word target for a piece, or for today's session.
- You want spelling mistakes underlined as you type.

## How to use it

### Focus mode

1. Open a Markdown or text note and choose **View** ▸ **Focus Mode** (<kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd>).
2. Panels and toolbars disappear and the text sits in a centred column.
3. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> or the same shortcut to leave. You come back to the same place.

Settings ▸ Editing sets the column width. **View** ▸ **Typewriter Scrolling** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd>) keeps the line you are typing in the middle of the screen.

### Writing goals

1. Choose **Edit** ▸ **Set Writing Goal…**, or click the word count in the status bar.
2. Set a target for this note, or for this session in this window.
3. Progress shows in the status bar and in focus mode.

A note's goal is saved in the note as a `word_goal` property. A session goal lasts until you close the window. **Clear Writing Goals** in <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> removes them.

### Status bar

The status bar follows the pane you are typing in: cursor line and column, selection size, word count, reading time (at 230 words a minute) and whether the note is saved. Click the cursor position to go to a line. Hide it with **View** ▸ **Show Status Bar**.

### Spelling

**Edit** ▸ **Check Spelling While Typing** underlines misspelled words in Markdown and text notes using the macOS spelling checker. Right-click a word for suggestions, to ignore it for now, or to learn it into your Mac's dictionary. Code, web addresses and front matter are not checked.

## What it will not do

- Focus mode changes only what you see. It never moves your cursor or selection.
- Spelling never changes a word by itself.
